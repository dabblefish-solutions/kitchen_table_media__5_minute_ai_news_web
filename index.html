OpenAI stops training frontier models on the live internet

OpenAI is building an offline testing environment using cached web content to train its models. This structural shift isolates autonomous AI, preventing it from disrupting real-world web infrastructure before release.

Google updated its privacy policy to permit ad targeting using personal Gmail and Photos data. Users must now trade historical privacy protections to access Google's personalized AI capabilities.
